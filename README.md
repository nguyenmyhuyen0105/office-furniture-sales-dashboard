# Business Performance Dashboard – Office Furniture Sales
## Live Dashboard

[View Interactive Power BI Dashboard](https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/46e9c9a2-2a6e-415c-aa4a-76c12c4b8765/4140a41001e4905a4a48?experience=power-bi)

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for analyzing the sales performance of an office furniture business from **September 2023 to September 2024**.

The dashboard provides an overview of revenue, orders, customers, order status, shipping methods, and monthly sales performance.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
* Track the number of orders and customers.
* Monitor order completion and cancellation rates.
* Compare revenue across different products.
* Analyze orders by shipping method.
* Identify monthly sales and revenue trends.
* Evaluate average customer ratings.

## Key KPIs

* **Total Orders:** 9,999
* **Completion Rate:** 67%
* **Revenue:** 2.478M

## Dashboard Features

### Revenue by Product

Compares revenue generated by different office furniture products, including:

* Office Chair
* Office Desk
* Storage Cabinet
* Bookshelf
* Desk Lamp

### Order by Status

Shows the distribution of:

* Completed orders
* Cancelled orders

### Order by Shipping Method

Analyzes orders based on shipping methods such as:

* Express
* Overnight
* Standard

### Orders and Revenue by Order Date

Visualizes daily order volume and revenue trends throughout the selected period.

### Monthly/Daily Performance

Provides detailed performance metrics by year and month, including:

* Number of customers
* Number of orders
* Revenue
* Completion rate
* Average customer rating

## Key Insights

The dashboard shows that completed orders account for approximately **67%** of total orders. Revenue is primarily generated by the main office furniture categories, while shipping performance can be compared across Express, Overnight, and Standard services.

The monthly breakdown also allows users to identify changes in customer activity, order volume, revenue, and completion rate over time.
